# Runtime-NFR configuration robustness results

- The frozen configuration reproduced all 8,668/8,668 routes.
- 540 prespecified configurations were evaluated.
- Route migration from the frozen configuration ranged from 0.0% to 24.8% (median 11.2%).
- Stakeholder-review-set Jaccard similarity ranged from 0.646 to 1.000 (median 0.819).
- Non-numeric disposition proportion ranged from 44.3% to 55.7% (median 49.3%).

These ranges describe sensitivity of the versioned routing policy to plausible configuration choices. They do not identify an optimal setting or establish cross-organizational validity.
